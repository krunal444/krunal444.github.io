Who we are

Sayline is made by KTeck ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your privacy, email krunalsoni060@gmail.com.

What Sayline uses, and where it stays

Sayline makes captions with a speech model that runs on your phone. Everything below is kept in Sayline's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Videos you pick

You choose videos with Android's photo picker, so Sayline can see only the videos you pick, not the rest of your gallery. It reads them where they are and keeps permission to read them again, so a project can reopen its video.

Videos you share to Sayline

A video you share to Sayline from another app is copied into Sayline's private storage, so its project keeps working after the other app lets go of it.

Recordings

Record uses your camera and microphone only while you are recording. A take stays in Sayline's private storage, and goes to your gallery only if you choose Save to Photos. Sayline never captions live while recording.

Audio, speech and captions

Sayline takes the audio from your video and turns speech into captions on your phone. Where it offers translation into English, that runs on your phone too. Your captions, transcripts, projects and their search index stay on your phone.

Your styles, Brand kit and words

Caption styles, the fonts and logos you add to your Brand kit, your list of words (My words) and your settings stay on your phone.

Exports

When you export, Sayline saves the video to Movies/Sayline and subtitle files to Download/Sayline on your phone. There, like any other video or file, they can be seen by your gallery, your file manager and other apps you have allowed to see your media. Sayline shares a video, a subtitle file or a privacy receipt image only when you choose an app in Android's share sheet. What that app does with it is covered by that app's own policy.

Notifications and widgets

Notifications show a job's progress, its project's title and a frame from your video, and may appear on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings. Sayline's home-screen widgets, if you add them, show your recent projects' titles and thumbnails on your home screen.

What leaves your phone

The Accurate speech model download

Sayline comes with its Fast speech model. If you choose to download the Accurate model, Sayline downloads it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over an encrypted HTTPS connection and checks the file before using it. This is the only time Sayline connects to the internet. The request contains nothing about you, your videos or your captions. Like any download, it shows Hugging Face your IP address and basic connection details, which Hugging Face handles under its privacy policy.

Feedback you send

"Send feedback" opens your email app with our address and a subject line; nothing is attached. If you send the email, we receive what you write and your email address. We use them only to reply to you and to improve Sayline, and we delete them if you ask.

Nothing else

Sayline has no account, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and no third-party code that sends data. We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't collect any.

Backups

Sayline opts out of Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so its projects are not copied to your Google account or to a new phone.

Google Play

If you installed Sayline from Google Play, Google handles that under Google's privacy policy. If you let your phone share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may show us aggregated, anonymous crash and performance statistics for Sayline. Sayline doesn't send these itself.

Sayline doesn't ask for access to all your photos, videos or files. The photo picker gives it only the videos you choose.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Everything Sayline keeps stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Delete a project in Sayline to delete its captions and any copy of its video. Settings ▸ Storage shows what Sayline keeps and can clear its cache. Settings ▸ Models deletes the downloaded Accurate model.
  • Uninstalling Sayline deletes everything in its private storage.
  • Exported videos and subtitle files in Movies/Sayline and Download/Sayline are your files. They stay until you delete them, even after Sayline is uninstalled.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, apart from any email you send us, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. What Sayline keeps is on your phone, under your control. To see or delete emails you have sent us, write to krunalsoni060@gmail.com.

Children

Sayline is not directed at children under 13. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Security

Sayline's files live in its private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. The model download uses HTTPS, and the file is checked against a known checksum before Sayline uses it.

Changes to this policy

If Sayline ever changes what it does with your data, this policy changes first. The new version is posted on this page with its effective date.
